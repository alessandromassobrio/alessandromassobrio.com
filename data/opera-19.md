## Info
<strong>Notes</strong>

A commission by ensemble mosaik for the UpTopThree Festival.

H+He - hydrogen and helium, the two lightest and most primordial elements — takes as its starting point an exploration of the sonic qualities of the instruments’ physical materials: the instrument is made to resonate as a physical body, rather than as a producer of pitches. The focus is on direct contact with the material, wood and metal as vibrating bodies. This gives rise to a functional repositioning of music in relation to the gesture, from which music emerges as a residue. Every sound event is the trace, the side-effect of a physical action; the gesture (a tactile, more or less complex parametric object) becomes the carrier of sound. In this microsystem, the damping of the discernible pitch takes precedence; harmony is determined by density; melody evaporates on the subjective level of momentary hearing and memory: it appears for an instant and vanishes, an involuntary by-product of what the performer’s body accomplishes on the instrument.
The gestures, however, are not islands: the piece treats them as sound objects on a transitional level, articulated in macro-modules. The music does not appear and disappear, but unfolds continuously within the trio, what emerges and subsides are rather simple configurational states that follow one another and merge into one another. The result is a fragile music born of thresholds rather than events, in which it is not the note that counts, but the moment when one state, for an instant, becomes another.
