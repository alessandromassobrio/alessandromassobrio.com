## Info
<strong>Notes</strong>

"In philosophy, systems theory, science, and art, emergence occurs when an entity is observed to have properties its parts do not have on their own. These properties or behaviors emerge only when the
parts interact in a wider whole." (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergence)
The general concept of this piece is simple: a number of given interchangable percussive actions are controlled/performed across the body of a detuned flat e-guitar (the sound object). The score may be played in all directionalities and/or with any combination of bars/modules, except for the first measure and the closing group of measures. The interaction between the magnetic poles of the pickups and the battery surface, the undetermined vibrations triggered by the scordatura, the amplified resonances and feedbacks create a relatively wide range of sound qualities, from dark to resonant, from bright to dull. One of the aim of the performative action is to create relatively complex sound structures starting from single gestures or entry points. Therefore the performer* should think and listen at gestures in terms of sound boundaries. This piece is a part of an ongoing composition for ensemble featuring, among the electric guitar, ocarinas, flutes a coulisse, strings, percussion and piano. 

E-guitar has to be played flat on performer's legs, or a supporting surface. Depending on the surface, it is recommended to place some foam rubber under the guitar, in order to dump unwanted vibrations. The piece can be performed by both a guitarist and a percussionist. 

By the material of the surface itself. The piece can be performed by both a guitarist and a percussionist. Part consists of three staves:
- a 6-lines stave, for the open strings. 
- a single-line stave, for the percussive actions (hand, mallet or battery). 
- a 2-line stave, for effect unit and volume control. 

Remarks:
This piece needs to be performed with the aid of an Electro Harmonix Holiest Grail. A different reverb unit would be suitable only by analysing and adapting the instructions accordingly. Playing techniques are designed for a Fender Stratocaster standard model, as for its shape, material, number (3) and type (single coil) of pickups and the quality of their magnets. For the best performance it
is recommended the use a model with similar features. Other guitars models are alsosuitable but there might be inconsistencies in the notation and sound.
